# Fees and commissions - Consumer loans (valid in 2026)

## Origination fees

| Fee type | Value |
|---|---|
| File analysis fee | 100 RON |
| Origination fee | 0.5% of the loan amount, minimum 50 RON |
| Monthly administration fee | 0.10% of the current balance |

## Fees during the loan term

| Fee type | Value |
|---|---|
| Partial prepayment fee | 1% of the prepaid amount |
| Full prepayment fee | 1% of the remaining balance |
| Due-date change fee | 35 RON per operation |
| In-branch account statement fee | 5 RON |
| Duplicate contract fee | 30 RON |

## Penalties

| Situation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Installment paid late, 1-30 days | 0.08% per day of the overdue installment |
| Installment paid late, over 30 days | 0.12% per day of the overdue installment |

NOTE: Compared to the 2025 fee schedule, the prepayment fee has decreased from 1.5% to 1%.
Valid exclusively for new contracts signed starting 01.01.2026.
